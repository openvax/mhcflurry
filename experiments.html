Experiment log

Dated empirical studies, cross-species validation campaigns, and controlled ablation experiments supporting MHCflurry releases.

2.5 series

  • Experiment 2026-11-25: Speculative pan-class Class I → Class II transfer via mhcseqs IMGT alignments (2026-11-25) — Speculative zero-shot Class I → Class II transfer test: mapping Class II DR/DQ/DP heterodimers into MHCflurry’s 39-aa groove representation via mhcseqs pan-class IMGT G-DOMAIN alignments and benchmarking frozen Class I 2.3.0 weights against NetMHCIIpan-4.3 and an HLA-A*02:01 negative control across 15 alleles (7,500 15-mer IC50 pairs).

  • Experiment 2026-11-25: Cross-species hitlist validation & mhcseqs training parity (2026-11-25) — Zero-shot Class I Affinity and Presentation (+ real species proteome flanks) validation across 11 previously unseen vertebrate species and 3 binding-pocket mutants using hitlist + IEDB, taxonomic expansion census (+184 net new species in mhcseqs), and controlled A/B pan-allele training parity (Max |Δ| = 0.0, r = 1.0) validating the GENERATE.sh --lock-existing migration.

2.3 series

  • Evaluation of the 2.3.0 weights & Detailed comparison of the 2.3.0 weights — Head-to-head benchmarks of the released 2.3.0 PyTorch weights against historical 2.2.0 weights and external predictors.

  • DLA evidence and canine evaluation — Exploratory canine DLA-88 evaluation and training evidence audit.

  • Reproduce the 2.3.0 model training — Clean-checkout replay of the 2.3.0 pan-allele release training recipe.

Additional working ablation notes (cleavage-boundary sweeps, kernel-width sweeps, and historical public-data replays) live in the repository’s notes/ directory.
