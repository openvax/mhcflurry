# MHCflurry 2.5.19

Documentation simplification and upgraded scientific negative controls in the
2.5 cross-species whitepaper. Zero changes to runtime code, `2.3.0` weights, or
predictions.

## Simpler User Guides & Cleaner Navigation

- **New everyday User guides:**
  - `docs/interpreting_predictions.md`: plain-English guide to every output
    column (`presentation_score`, `presentation_percentile`, `affinity`,
    `affinity_percentile`, `processing_score`, `best_allele`), which score to
    rank by, practical cutoffs, genotypes vs. single alleles, and flanks vs. no
    flanks.
  - `docs/scanning_proteins.md`: practical recipes for `mhcflurry predict-scan`,
    filtering (`--results-best`, `--threshold-affinity`, `--results-all`),
    memory-bounded proteome streaming with `--chunk-size`, and common CLI
    pitfalls (shell quoting, `;` vs. space allele arguments, `--no-throw`).
- **Leaner top-level sidebar:** Moved `cross_species_validation`, `training`,
  `evaluation`, and `release_model_evaluation` into **Advanced topics**, so
  **User guides** contains strictly everyday prediction workflows.
- **Trimmed contributor internals:** Rewrote `docs/orchestrator.md` from 441
  lines of repetitive notes down to a concise 68-line reference, removed its
  stale `orphan: true` tag, and nested both `orchestrator` and
  `auto_sizing_audit` under `docs/maintainers.md`.

## Upgraded Negative Controls in the Cross-Species Whitepaper & PDF

Replaced the single `HLA-A*02:01` negative-control allele throughout
`docs/whitepaper_25x_cross_species.md`, `docs/cross_species_validation.md`,
and `docs/_static/whitepaper/mhcflurry_25x_cross_species_whitepaper.pdf` (now
featuring 16 standalone single-panel vector figures) with three complementary
controls:

- **50-allele mismatched real-allele null distributions** (excluding the target
  species; size-matched `k`-allele random draws for multi-allelic haplotypes
  such as Tasmanian devil `Saha` and Cattle `BoLA-I`), reporting median,
  5th–95th percentile band, and empirical *p*-values.
- **30-groove column-permuted pseudosequences** (shuffling each of the 39
  positions independently across real alleles).
- **Within-peptide residue-scrambled positive controls** (real ligands vs.
  composition-matched shuffled copies of those exact ligands): confirms genuine
  positional anchor decoding across feline, avian, marsupial, primate, cattle,
  and pocket-mutant Class I cohorts (`+0.20` to `+0.29` AUROC over mismatched
  alleles), while proving that frozen Class I 9-mer core scanning on Class II
  binders collapses to a **`0.502`** coin flip once amino-acid composition is
  held constant (versus **`0.725`** for `NetMHCIIpan-4.3 BA`).
