# MHCflurry 2.5.8

Adds a published **Experiment log** section to the documentation (with the
complete dated `2.5.x` cross-species `hitlist` + IEDB validation report, 16-row
summary table, and new publication-ready groove-specificity & A/B training
parity synthesis figure) and adds a defensive guard against bare UniProt
accessions being misparsed as MHC alleles (`pirl-unc/mhcgnomes#306`). Existing
`2.3.0` model weights and predictions are unchanged.

## Highlights

- **Defensive UniProt accession guard (`pirl-unc/mhcgnomes#306`):**
  - `_normalize_pseudosequence_allele_key()` in
    `mhcflurry/mhcseqs_pseudosequences.py` now skips bare 6-/10-character
    UniProtKB accessions (without `-`, `*`, or `:`) before invoking
    `mhcgnomes`, preventing Japanese medaka fish accessions
    (`H2LR16`–`H2LR44`) from being misparsed as mouse `H2-L*r16`–`r44` alleles
    or `P#####` accessions from being parsed as human pseudogene `HLA-P*`.
    Raw accessions remain available when `normalize_alleles=False`.
- **Published Experiment log & cross-species synthesis figure:**
  - Added `docs/experiments.md` and
    `docs/experiment_25x_cross_species_hitlist.md` (linked from `docs/index.md`
    and `docs/cross_species_validation.md`) recording the complete 16-cohort
    unseen-species & pocket-mutant validation study, flanked `hitlist` MS
    Presentation benchmarks, controlled A/B pan-allele training parity proof,
    and upstream data-hygiene audit (`pirl-unc/mhcseqs#141`,
    `pirl-unc/mhcgnomes#306`, `pirl-unc/hitlist#977`).
  - Added `docs/_static/release-2.5.8/fig4_zeroshot_vs_control_and_parity.svg`
    comparing target zero-shot AUROC against `HLA-A*02:01` negative controls
    across all 16 unseen-species & mutant cohorts alongside the 3-arm A/B
    training parity results.
